### 3. Competitive Velocity Indicators
*   **Search Volume Trends:** Google Trends data indicates a shift in "Performance" searches. Queries comparing *iQOO 13 vs iPhone 15* have increased 22% in the last quarter, signaling consumer curiosity in replacing expensive flagships with high-spec Androids.
*   **Social Media Engagement:** OPPO Find X8 leads in "Premium Perception" hashtags and aesthetic-based content engagement, while iQOO 13 dominates "Tech-Tuber" reviews and gaming-centric community sentiment.
*   **Web Traffic Patterns:** Traffic to the iQOO 13 product landing page spikes aggressively during sale events, confirming a high-elasticity, "deal-seeking" buyer profile.
*   **Sales Ranking Changes:** The Realme GT 7 Pro has seen a rapid climb in "Bestseller" lists upon release, though velocity tends to taper off within 60 days, suggesting shorter product lifecycle focus compared to Apple's sustained demand.

### 4. Gaining vs. Losing Competitors
*   **Top 3 Gaining:**
    1.  **iQOO:** Gaining via "Performance-First" dominance; successfully peeling off tech-savvy users from aging iPhones.
    2.  **Google (Pixel series):** Gaining share in the "AI-premium" niche by emphasizing exclusive software features over raw hardware specs.
    3.  **Vivo (X-Series):** Gaining by leveraging premium camera partnerships (Zeiss) to capture the "prosumer" segment.
*   **Top 3 Losing:**
    1.  **Realme:** Losing share in the premium segment due to brand dilution; struggling to move buyers up from budget models to the GT Pro price tier.
    2.  **iPhone 15 (Base):** Losing "Share of Mind" among power users; the device is increasingly seen as "under-spec'd" (60Hz/6GB RAM) for its price.
    3.  **OnePlus:** Losing traction due to software sentiment shifts and aggressive competition from the iQOO/Vivo performance duopoly.

### 5. Market Share Drivers & Opportunities
*   **Key Drivers of Share Gains:**
    *   **Hardware Leapfrogging:** Providing 12GB+ RAM, 120Hz LTPO displays, and Periscope lenses at ~₹20k–₹30k below the cost of an "iPhone Pro" equivalent.
    *   **AI-Specific Value:** Integrating NPU-heavy processors (SD 8 Elite/Dimensity 9400) to market future-proof capabilities.
*   **Potential Share Capture Opportunities:**
    *   **The "iPhone Switcher" Program:** High intent among iPhone 13/14 owners to upgrade; these users are currently dissatisfied with the incremental updates of the iPhone 15/16.
    *   **Tier 2/3 Market Expansion:** Investing in localized, high-performance retail experiences to capture rural/semi-urban "aspirational" buyers.
*   **Vulnerabilities of Leading Competitors:**
    *   **Apple:** Over-reliance on "Ecosystem Lock-in." If Android competitors successfully simplify data migration (Cloud/App syncing), the barrier to exit shrinks.
    *   **Realme/iQOO:** "Software Bloat." The perception that these devices are cluttered compared to iOS prevents mass-market, high-spending users from switching.
